The Spirit of Sindh

Sindh Pakistan ka ek bohot hi tareekhi, khoobsurat aur mehmaan-nawaz subah hai, jis ki tareekh hazaron saal hai. Yeh zameen qadeem Indus Valley Civilization ka markaz rahi hai, jahan Mohenjo-Daro jaisa azeem tareekhi muqam maujood hai. Sindh ko Bab-ul-Islam bhi kaha jata hai kyun ke bar-e-sagheer mein Islam aamad isi sar-zameen se hui thi. Yahan ki saqafat aur riwayaat poori duniya mein apni ek alag pehchan rakhti hain. Sindh ka Ajrak aur embroidered Sindhi Topi yahan ke waqar aur tehzeeb ki bohot badi alamat hain. Darya-e-Sindh (Indus River) is pooray subay ke zariye behta hai aur yahan ki zeraat, tijarat aur zindagani ki sab se badi wajah hai. Sindh ki zameen Sufiya-e-Karam ki zameen hai, jahan Shah Abdul Latif Bhittai aur Lal Shahbaz Qalandar jaise azeem buzurgon ne aman, mohabbat aur bhaichare ka paigham diya. Yahan ke log apni bey-had warm hospitality aur narm-dili ke liye pure mulk mein jaane jaate hain. Karachi, jo Sindh ka dar-ul-hukoomat hai, Pakistan ka sab se bada shehar aur azeem maashi (economic) markaz hai. Sehwan, Hyderabad, Sukkur aur Larkana jaise shehar Sindh ke tareekhi aur saqafati husn mein mazeed izafa karte hain. Sindh ki zameen par moosam aur kudrat ke alag hi rang dekhne ko milte hain, jahan ek taraf darya aur jheelein hain toh doosri taraf Thar ka haseen sehra hai. Yahan ka khana, jaise Sindhi Biryani aur traditional mithaiyan, poore mulk mein shoq se khaye jaate hain. Sindh ki sar-zameen ne hamesha ilm, adab, aur tehzeeb ko aage barhaya hai. Yeh qadeem tareekh aur jadeed daur ka ek behtareen aur haseen sangam hai. Overall, Sindh Pakistan ka ek anmol nagina hai jis par poore mulk ko fakhar hai.

.1 Karachi:

Yahan ki sab se bari khas baat yeh hai ke yeh shehar kabhi sota nahi hai, raat ke late waqt bhi yahan rasta aur market open milti hai aur raunaq rehti hai. Yahan ki aab-o-hawa mein samandar ki wajah se sham ke waqt bohot pyari thandi hawa chalti hai jo din bhar ki thakan door kar deti hai. Karachi ke log bohot mehmaan-nawaz, friendly aur mehnat-kash hain jo har aane wale ka khule dil se istaqbal karte hain. Yahan street food se le kar bade restaurants tak har tarah ka chatpata aur mazaydar khana milta hai. Yeh shehar har kisi ko aage badhne aur apna khwab poora karne ka mauqa deta hai.
