Land of Five Rivers

Punjab Bharat ka ek behad khoobsurat, aitihasik aur sanskritik roop se samriddh rajya hai jo apni mehman-nawazi aur zinda-dili ke liye poore desh mein jana jata hai. 'Punjab' shabd ka arth "Paanch Nadiyon Ki Bhoomi" hota hai, jo yahan behne wali paanch mukhya nadiyon ko darshata hai. Yeh rajya apni upjaau zameen aur krishi ke liye prasiddh hai, jiski wajah se ise Bharat ka "Ann Bhandar" bhi kaha jata hai. Yahan ke sarson ke khet, haryali aur shandar vatavaran har kisi \ka dil jeet lete hain. Sikh dharam ki paavan virasat aur Amritsar ka Golden Temple is rajya ko poori duniya mein ek khas dharmik pehchan dilaate hain. Punjab ke log behad mehnat-kash, friendly aur khule dil ke hote hain jo har aane wale ka dil se swagat karte hain. Yahan ka Bhangra lok nritya, Lohri aur Baisakhi jaise tyohar poore desh mein dhoom-dham se manaye jaate hain. Khane-peene ke mamle mein bhi Punjab sabse aage hai, jahan Makki ki Roti, Sarson ka Saag, desi makkhan aur meethi Lassi poore Bharat mein pasand ki jaati hai. Ludhiana, Amritsar aur Jalandhar jaise bade shahar is rajya ki aarthik aur sanskritik shaan hain. Punjab sirf ek rajya nahi balki ek anutha anubhav hai jo har kisi ko apnapan aur sukoon deta hai.

    • .1Amritsar:

    ​Amritsar Punjab ka sab se mashhoor, aitihasik aur pavitra shahar hai jo Bharat aur Pakistan ki border ke paas sthit hai. Yeh shahar poore Punjab ki dharmik aur sanskritik pehchan ka sab se main kendra hai aur ise Sikh dharam ki rajdhani bhi kaha jata hai. Yahan ki sab se badi khas baat yeh hai ke yeh shahar har dharam, har jaat aur har jagah se aane wale logon ka khule dil se istaqbal karta hai. Yahan har saal duniya bhar se lakho log darshan karne aur yahan ki khoobsurat virasat ko dekhne aate hain. Amritsar mein padhai, business, kapde ki market aur kapda industry ke sath-sath purane aur famous bazaars majoord hain jo din raat logon ki chahal-pahal aur raunaq se bhare rehte hain. Yahan ke log bohot mehman-nawaz, friendly aur zinda-dil hain jo har aane wale ka dil se swagat karte hain aur apne swadist khane aur pyaar se sabhi ka dil jeet lete hain. Yahan street food se le kar bade dhabon tak har tarah ka chatpata aur mazaydar Amritsari Kulcha, meethi Lassi aur shandar mithaiyan milti hain. Amritsar sirf ek shahar nahi hai balki ek aisi jagah hai jo har kisi ko shanti, sukoon, ekta aur aitihasik virasat ka behtareen anubhav karwati hai.

    .Golden Temple:

    ​Golden Temple Amritsar ki sab se ziada famous aur paavan ghoomne wali jagah hai jahan duniya bhar se log sukoon aur darshan ke liye aate hain. Yeh shandar gurudwara ek pavitra sarovar ke beech mein bana hua hai aur iski deewaron par sone ki khoobsurat parat chadhi hui hai. Yahan din ho ya raat, hamesha shradhaluon aur paryatakon ka mela laga rehta hai aur gurbani ki meethi aawaz se poora vatavaran pavitra lagta hai. Yahan duniya ki sab se badi muft rasoi (Langar) chalti hai jahan har roz bina kisi bhedbhav ke hazaron log ek sath zameen par baith kar khana khate hain. Raat ke waqt jab lights chalti hain toh sarovar ke paani mein sone ke mandir ka parchhaai aur manzar aur bhi pyaara lagta hai. Yahan aakar logon ka mind fresh ho jata hai aur man ko behad shanti milti hai. Golden Temple ke darshan kiye bina Amritsar ka trip hamesha adhoora samjha jata hai.

    .Jallianwala Bagh:

    Jallianwala Bagh Golden Temple ke bilkul paas sthit ek behad prasiddh aur aitihasik park hai jahan log Bharat ki aazadi ke itihas ko samajhne aur dekhne aate hain. Yeh jagah 1919 ke shahidon ki yaad mein banayi gayi hai jahan British daur mein General Dyer ne hazaron nishatthey logon par andhadhund goliya chalwayi thin. Park mein ghoomte hue aaj bhi purani deewaron par goliyon ke nishan saaf dekhe ja sakte hain jo us daur ki dardnak kahani batate hain. Shahidon ki yaad mein jalti hui Amar Jyoti aur wo aitihasik kuan, jismein log jaan bachane ke liye kood gaye the, is jagah ko aur bhi khas aur aitihasik banata hai. Log yahan aakar apne freedom fighters ko shraddhanjali dete hain aur Bharat ke itihas ke bare mein jaante hain. Shahar ke beech mein hone ki wajah se yahan hamesha touriston ki aawadajahi rehti hai aur yeh Amritsar ke sab se mahatvapurna tourist spots mein se ek hai

    .Wagah Border:

    Wagah Border Bharat aur Pakistan ke beech ki antarrashtriya seema par sthit ek behad romanchak ghoomne wali jagah hai jo Amritsar shahar se lagbhag 30 kilometer ki doori par hai. Har sham ko is border par BSF ke jawan aur Pakistani sena dwara ek shandar "Beating Retreat" ceremony ki jaati hai. Is parade aur ceremony ko dekhne ke liye roz hazaron ki sankhya mein log door-door se ikatha hote hain aur pura stadium logon se packed rehta hai. Log "Bharat Mata Ki Jai" aur deshbhakti ke naare lagate hain, jis se wahan ka mahaul ekdam josh se bhar jata hai. Sainikon ki shandar parade, unka strict discipline aur aasmaan mein tiranga lehrate hue dekhna har tourist ke liye ek unforgettable anubhav hota hai. Amritsar aane wale har insaan ke liye Wagah Border ki sair karna aur deshbhakti ka josh anubhav karna ek dam best option hota hai.

    .2Ludhiana:

    Ludhiana Punjab ka sab se bada aur aabad shahar hai jo Sutlej nadi ke kinare sthit hai. Yeh shahar poore desh mein apni textile industry, kapda market aur hosiery ke kaam ke liye jana jata hai, jiski wajah se ise Punjab ki industrial rajdhani bhi kaha jata hai. Yahan har kism ke kapde, cycle aur machine parts tayyar hote hain jo poore Bharat ke sath-sath videshon mein bhi export kiye jaate hain. Shahar mein har tarah ke business, bade commercial centers, top colleges aur medical institutes majoord hain. Ludhiana ke log bohot mehnat-kash, friendly aur zinda-dil hain jo din raat apne kaam aur business ko aage badhane mein lage rehte hain. Khane-peene ke mamle mein bhi yeh shahar kisi se kam nahi hai, yahan ke dhabon ka desi khana, makki ki roti aur sarson ka saag behad prasiddh hai. Shahar ke bazaars aur malls hamesha logon ki chahal-pahal se bhare rehte hain. Ludhiana ek aisa shahar hai jo rozgar aur taraqqui ke naye mauqe deta hai aur Punjab ki economy ka sab se main hissa samjha jata hai.

    Nehru Rose Garden Ludhiana ka ek behad khoobsurat aur bada botanical garden hai jo hazaaron kism ke gulab ke phoolon ke liye jana jata hai. Park mein khoobsurat musical fountains aur jogging tracks banaye gaye hain jo ise aur bhi akarshak banate hain.     .3 Jalandhar:

    ​Jalandhar Punjab ka ek behad purana, aitihasik aur mahatvapurna shahar hai jo apni sports industry aur educational institutes ke liye poore desh mein jana jata hai. Yeh shahar sports ke saman (sports goods) ki manufacturing ka sab se bada kendra hai, jahan se cricket bat, football aur hockey sticks poori duniya mein export kiye jaate hain. Iske alawa, Jalandhar media, newspaper printing aur IT companies ke liye bhi Punjab mein ek mukhya hub samjha jata hai. Shahar mein top engineering aur medical colleges majoord hain jo har saal hazaaron students ko aakarshit karte hain. Yahan ke log bohot mehnat-kash, friendly aur zinda-dil hain jo mehman-nawazi mein koi kasar nahi chhodte. Khane-peene ke mamle mein bhi yeh shahar behad prasiddh hai, jahan street food se le kar traditional Punjabi dhabon tak har tarah ka laziz khana milta hai. Jalandhar ki markets aur bazaars hamesha rounq aur logon ki chahal-pahal se bhare rehte hain.
