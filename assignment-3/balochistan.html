Balochistan Guide

Balochistan Pakistan ka raqbay ke lihaaz se sab se bada aur qudrati wasail se malamal sooba hai. Yeh sooba apni azeem pahadi shron, gahri vaadiyon aur lambay samundari kinaron ke liye poori dunya mein jaana jata hai. Satah samandar se buland hone ki waja se iske bohot se ilaqon mein mausam behad khushgawar aur thanda rehta hai. Is soobay ki sarhadain Afghanistan aur Iran se milti hain, jo ise tijarati lihaaz se behad ahem banati hain. Balochistan mein alag alag saqafaton ka behtareen milaap dekhne ko milta hai jahan Balochi, Pashtun aur Brahvi zabanain boli jaati hain. Yahan ke log apni mehman nawazi, riwayaat aur bahaduri ke liye behad prasiddh hain. Soobay ka darul hukoomat Quetta hai, jise apni khoobsoorati aur phalon ke baghaat ki waja se "Fruit Garden" kaha jata hai. Arab Sagar ke kinare waqia Gwadar Port is soobay ko aalmi tijarat ka sab se main markaz banati hai. Ziarat ka ilaka apne hazaron saal purane Sanobar (Juniper) ke jungles aur aitihaasik Ziarat Residency ke liye mashhoor hai. Balochistan ke baazaaron mein haath se bani hui shandaar balochi embroidery aur zewaraat touriston ko aakarshit karte hain. Yahan ka mashhoor khana Balochi Sajji aur Rosh poore desh mein behad zaiqedaar samjha jata hai. Soobay mein qudrati koyla, gas aur minerals behad zyaada miqdaar mein majood hain jo mulk ki economy mein bada yogdan dete hain. Hingol National Park aur Kund Malir Beach jaise anokhe qudrati manazir is soobay ki shaan hain. Yahan ke registan, retai teele aur gahre samundar ek sath mil kar ek anokha qudrati manzar pesh karte hain.Official Website Sardi ke mausam mein pahadon par hone wali barfbari touriston ke liye ek behad yaadgar anubhav hoti hai. Soobay mein taleem aur vikas ke naye mauqe tezi se badh rahe hain jo iske bhavishya ko roshan banate hain. Yahan ki purani tareekh aur qadeem rehaish ke nishan aaj bhi aitihaasik shauqeen logon ko kheench laate hain. Balochistan ki saaf hawa aur shanti bhara mahaul shehar ki bhag-daud se door sukoon deta hai. Yeh sooba apni qudrati khubsoorati, ahem tijarati location aur aala saqafat ki waja se poore mulk ki shaan aur pehchan hai.

.1 Quetta

Quetta Balochistan ka darul hukoomat aur sab se bada shehar hai jo khoobsoorat pahadon ke darmayan waqia hai. Yeh shehar satah samandar se taqreeban 1,680 meter ki bulandi par basa hua hai. Charon taraf se pahadon se gheray hone ki waja se ise ek qudrati qila bhi kaha jata hai. Yahan ka mausam saal ke zyada tar hissay mein khushgawar aur thanda rehta hai. Quetta ko poore mulk mein "Fruit Garden of Pakistan" ke naam se jana jata hai. Yahan ke baghaat ke taaza seb, anar aur khoobani poore desh mein supply kiye jatay hain. Yeh shehar apni ahem jugrafeeyai (geographical) location ki waja se tijarat ka ek bada markaz hai. Yahan Balochi, Pashtun aur Hazara saqafat ka behtareen milaap dekhne ko milta hai. Shehar ke log behad mehman nawaz aur apni riwayaat se juray hue hain. Yahan ki bazaaron mein haath se bani hui zewaraat aur kapde touriston ko aakarshit karte hain. Sardi ke mausam mein pahadon par hone wali barfbari shehar ki khoobsoorati mein chaar chaand laga deti hai. Quetta mein aitihaasik aur qudrati dono tarah ke ghoomne layak maqamat majood hain. Yeh shehar education aur healthcare ke lihaaz se bhi soobay ka sab se main markaz hai. Yahan ki mashhoor khurak Sajji aur Rosh poore Pakistan mein mashhoor hai. Sanobar ke jungle aur pakke pahadi raste is shehar ko ek alag pehchan dete hain. Yeh shehar har saal hazaron touriston ko apni taraf aakarshit karta hai.

.Khushgawar Mausam Aur Barfbari:

Quetta ka mausam saal ke zyada tar mahino mein behad khushgawar rehta hai. Garmiyon ke mausam mein jab doosre sheharon mein shdeed garmi hoti hai, tab bhi yahan ka mausam thanda aur sukoon dene wala hota hai. Sardi ke mausam mein yahan taapmaan kaafi neeche chala jata hai aur shdeed thand padti hai. Pahadon par jab safed barf ki chadar bichh jati hai to poore shehar ka manzar behad khoobsoorat ho jata hai. Barfbari dekhne ke liye door door se log Quetta ka rukh karte hain. Yahan ki taaza aur saaf hawa sehat ke lihaaz se bhi behad acchi samjhi jati hai. Shaam ke waqt pahadon par dhalte sooraj ka manzar dekhna ek anokha anubhav hota hai. Mausam ki is khususiyaat ki waja se Quetta ko Balochistan ka sab se mashhoor tourist spot mana jata hai. Log yahan apne pariwar ke saath sukoon ke pal bitane aate hain. Yeh mausam shehar ki khubsoorati ko poore saal zinda rakhta hai.

.Lazeez Khane Aur Sajji:

Quetta apne zaiqedaar aur khas khanon ke liye poori dunya mein ek alag pehchan rakhta hai. Yahan ka sab se mashhoor khana "Balochi Sajji" hai jo bakray ya murgh ke gosht se tayaar ki jati hai. Sajji ko halkay masalon ke saath lakdiyon ki aag par dheemi aanch par pakaya jata hai. Iske alawa "Rosh" ya Namkeen Gosht bhi yahan ka ek behad popular traditional dish hai. Khane ke shauqeen logon ke liye Quetta ki food streets ek jannat ki tarah hain. Yahan ka gosht apne khas zaiqe aur taza hone ki waja se jaana jata hai. Kahwa (Green Tea) yahan har khane ke baad peene ki purani riwayat hai jo hazme ke liye acchi hoti hai. Local hotels aur restaurants mein yahan ki mehman nawazi ka alag hi andaz dekhne ko milta hai. Yahan ke traditional khane packing karwaye bina tourist waapas nahi jaate. Lazeez khanon ki is variation ki waja se Quetta ka khana har kisi ka dil jeet leta hai.

.Phal Aur Dry Fruits:

Quetta ko "Fruit Garden" kaha jata hai kyun ke yahan har kism ke aala quality ke phal peda hote hain. Yahan ke seb, anar, baadaam, kishmish aur khoobani poore mulk mein behad prasiddh hain. Shehar ke chaaron taraf phailay baghaat iski economy aur khubsoorati mein bada yogdan dete hain. Yahan ke dry fruits ki quality sab se aala samjhi jati hai aur yeh poore desh mein export hote hain. Bazaaron mein dry fruits ki badi badi dukaanain hain jahan tourist khareedari karte hain. Khususi taur par yahan ke kaghzi baadaam aur dry khubaani behad pasand ki jati hai. Phalon ki fasal ke mausam mein poora shehar taaza phalon ki khushboo se mehak uthta hai. Yahan ke kisaan apni fasal ko behad mehnat aur qudrati tareeqon se ugate hain. Quetta aane wale log apne doston aur pariwar ke liye dry fruits ko tohfe ke taur par zaroor le jatay hain. Phal aur dry fruits Quetta ki pehchan ka ek behad zaroori hissa hain.

.2 Gwadar

Gwadar Balochistan ka ek behad ahem aur khoobsoorat coastal shehar hai jo Arab Sagar ke kinare waqia hai. Yeh shehar apne shandaar beachein aur aalami satah par ahem bandarghah (port) ki waja se jaana jata hai. CPEC project ke baad se Gwadar poore kshetra ka ek bada tijarti aur economic hub ban chuka hai. Yahan ka samundari mausam aur khoobsoorat manazir touriston ko apni taraf aakarshit karte hain. Shehar ke aaspas safed ret ke beachein aur safed pahad iski khoobsoorati mein izafa karte hain. Yahan ki machhli aur seafood poore soobay mein behad prasiddh hain. Gwadar ka silsila purane zamane se maritime trade aur saqafat se juda hua hai. Yahan ke log zyadatar machhli pakadne aur tijarat ke karobar se juray hain. Shehar mein naye hotels, resorts aur infrastructure tezi se vikasit ho rahe hain. Gwadar Port ki waja se yeh shehar aane wale waqt mein poore mulk ki economy ka sab se main hissa banne wala hai. Yahan ki taaza hawa aur samundari leharon ka shor sukoon dene wala hota hai. Touriston ke liye yeh ek behad khaas aur vishvasniya jagah hai. Gwadar ki tareekh aur iska aadhunik vikas ise ek alag pehchan deta hai. Yeh shehar Balochistan ki shaan aur bhavishya ka ek behtareen udaharan hai.

.Gwadar Port Aur CPEC Project:

Gwadar Port is shehar ki sab se badi pehchan hai jo gahre paani ki ek aalmi bandarghah hai. China-Pakistan Economic Corridor (CPEC) ke tehet is port ko international trade ka sab se main markaz banaya gaya hai. Is project ki waja se Gwadar mein naye raste, international airport aur behtareen infrastructure tayaar ho raha hai. Yeh port poore central Asia aur doosre deshon ko samundari raste se jodne ka kaam karta hai. Tijarti lihaaz se yeh jagah aane wale waqt mein hazaron naye naukriyon ke mauqe paida karegi. Gwadar Port ki waja se shehar mein aalmi satah par log aate jaate hain. Isne Gwadar ko ek chote machheron ke gaon se badal kar ek aalmi tijarti shehar banaya hai. Yeh project poore Balochistan ki economy ko ek naye disha mein le ja raha hai. Touriston ke liye Port area aur iska view point dekhna ek anokha anubhav hota hai. Yeh Gwadar ki vikas aur ahem haysiyat ki sab se badi wajah hai.

.Khoobsoorat Beachein Aur Hammerhead:

Gwadar apne khoobsoorat aur saaf-suthre samundari kinaron (beaches) ke liye poore mulk mein prasiddh hai. Yahan ka Hammerhead pahad samundar ke andar ek kripa jaise aakar mein phaila hua hai jo dekhne mein behad shandaar lagta hai. Gwadar Beach par dhalte sooraj ka manzar aur neela paani logon ka dil jeet leta hai. Yahan log boating, fishing aur samundar ke kinare walk karne ke liye aate hain. Pahadon aur samundar ka yeh anokha milaap duniya ke behad kam sheharon mein dekhne ko milta hai. Gwadar ke beaches par shanti aur sukoon ka mahaul rehta hai jo shehar ki bhag-daud se door le jata hai. Night time mein samundar ki hawa aur leharon ki aawaz behad khoobsoorat lagti hai. Tourist yahan apne family ke saath yaadgar waqt bitane ke liye aate hain. Yeh beaches Gwadar ki natural beauty ka sab se bada hissa hain.

.Seafood Aur Local Saqafat:

​Gwadar samundar ke kinare hone ki waja se aala quality ke fresh seafood ke liye jana jata hai. Yahan ke restaurants mein taaza machhli, prawn aur crab behad khas tareeqon aur local masalon se pakaye jatay hain. Local Balochi zaiqa aur samundari khanon ka combination khane ke shauqeen logon ke liye ek jannat ki tarah hai. Yahan ke machheron ki zindagi aur unki traditional boats (dhows) dekhna ek alag saqafati anubhav hai. Gwadar ke log behad mehman nawaz aur seedhe-saadhe hote hain. Bazaaron mein haath se bani hui crafts aur traditional kapde milte hain. Yahan ki machhli mandi mein subah ke waqt ki chahal-pahal dekhne layak hoti hai. Touriston ke liye local seafood khana Gwadar visit ka ek zaroori hissa ban chuka hai. Yeh khana aur saqafat Gwadar ko ek khas aur yaadgar pehchan dete hain.

.3 Ziarat:

Ziarat Balochistan ka ek behad mashhoor aur khoobsoorat hill station hai jo pahadon ke darmayan waqia hai. Yeh shehar apni thandak, haryali aur qudrati khubsoorati ki waja se poore mulk mein jaana jata hai. Satah samandar se kafi bulandi par hone ki waja se yahan ka mausam garmiyon mein bhi behad khushgawar rehta hai. Is shehar ko dunia ke sab se bade Sanobar (Juniper) ke jangaloo ka ghar bhi kaha jata hai. Ziarat ki hawa behad saaf aur sehat-bakhsh samjhi jati hai jo touriston ko taza-dam kar deti hai. Yeh shehar Quetta se zyaada door nahi hai, jis waja se log aasan se yahan visit karte hain. Yahan ki shaant vaadiyan aur pahadi raste logon ko sukoon aur anand dete hain. Ziarat mein aitihaasik aur qudrati dono tarah ke aakarshan majood hain. Sardi ke mausam mein yahan shdeed barfbari hoti hai jo poori vaadi ko khoobsoorat bana deti hai. Local log behad mehman nawaz hain aur unka rehen-sehen saada hai. Yahan ke apple orchards (seb ke baghaat) poore soobay mein mashhoor hain. Touriston ke liye yahan picnic, hiking aur camping ke behad acche mauqe milte hain. Ziarat Balochistan ka ek aisa heera hai jo har kisi ko apni taraf aakarshit karta hai. Yeh jagah khandani chhuttiyon aur sukoon ke pal bitane ke liye ek behtareen maqam hai.

.Qadeem Sanobar Ke Jungle:

Ziarat ki sab se badi pehchan yahan ke zamaana-e-qadeem ke Sanobar (Juniper) ke jungle hain. Yeh jungle hazaron saal purane hain aur duniya ke sab se bade Juniper forests mein shumaar hote hain. In darakhton ki khas baat yeh hai ke yeh behad dheemi raftaar se badhte hain aur saalon tak zinda rehte hain. In jungles ki waja se Ziarat ki hawa behad saaf aur khushboodar rehti hai. Yahan hiking aur walk karne se logon ko qudrat ke behad qareeb hone ka ehsaas hota hai. Yeh jungle bohot se nayaab parindon aur janwaron ka qudrati ghar bhi hain. Environment aur nature ke shauqeen logon ke liye yeh jagah ek jannat ki tarah hai. Government aur local log in qadeem darakhton ko mehfooz rakhne ke liye koshish karte hain. In jungles ki wajah se Ziarat ko ek khas qudrati pehchan mili hai. Touriston ke liye in darakhton ke beech waqt guzarna ek behad yaadgar anubhav hota hai.

.Aitihaasik Ziarat Residency:

Ziarat Residency is shehar ki sab se mashhoor aitihaasik imarat hai jahan Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah ne apne aakhri din guzare the. Yeh khoobsoorat lakdi se bani hui imarat apne shandaar architecture aur taareekhi haysiyat ke liye jaani jaati hai. Yeh residency sanobar ke darakhton aur phoolon ke baghaat ke darmayan waqia hai. Touriston ke liye yeh ek behad ahem aur zaroori point hai jahan log desh ki taareekh ko kareeb se dekhte hain. Residency ke andar Quaid-e-Azam ki istemaal ki hui cheezein aur purani tasveerein mehfooz ki gayi hain. Is imarat ka shaant mahaul aur surrounding view behad dilkash hai. Har saal hazaron log is taareekhi jagah ko dekhne aur shradhanjali dene aate hain. Yeh residency Ziarat ki shaan aur taareekhi pehchan ka sab se main hissa hai. Is jagah par aakar log desh ke itihaas aur Quaid ki zindagi ke baare mein jaante hain.

.Khushgawar Mausam Aur Sehat-Bakhsh Hawa::

Ziarat apne saal bhar rehne wale thande aur khushgawar mausam ke liye poore Balochistan mein mashhoor hai. Garmiyon ke dino mein jab doosre sheharon mein shdeed garmi hoti hai, tab Ziarat ka mausam behad thanda aur sukoon dene wala hota hai. Sardi ke dino mein yahan shdeed barfbari hoti hai aur poora shehar safed chadar mein dhak jata hai. Yahan ki hawa mein sanobar ke darakhton ki qudrati khushboo milti hai jo sehat ke liye behad faide-mand hoti hai. Bohot se log apni sehat aur tazgi ke liye yahan chhuttiyan bitane aate hain. Shaam ke waqt vaadiyon mein kohra (fog) chha jata hai jo manzar ko aur bhi rochak bana deta hai. Yahan ka shanti bhara mahaul shehar ki bhag-daud se door sukoon deta hai. Touriston ke liye yahan har mausam mein ek alag tarah ka qudrati anubhav milta hai.
